# Week 2 Summary

> **How can understanding our divine nature affect how we treat ourselves and others?**  
> *When have you had an experience where you have been able to look past someone’s offensive behavior or attitude and see their divine nature?*

---

## Reflection

When we understand our divine nature, it changes our perspective. It helps us look beyond physical limitations and see our true potential.

---

**Personal Struggles:**

- I sometimes struggle with self-doubt and pride.
- At times, I look down on others because I think I am better.
- But when I reflect carefully, I realize that we are all brothers and sisters in spirit, with the same Heavenly Parents.

---

**Christlike Example:**

> Just as our Savior ministered during His time, He treated everyone with equal compassion. To Him, we are all afflicted and in need of care and attention.

---

**Application:**

If we could think the same way as our Savior, it would be easier to:

- Forgive
- Extend help
- Sacrifice for those in need
